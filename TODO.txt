attempts table, for ip tracking middleware:
	when an 'Unauthorized' attempt is made to the /auth namespace, add ip to table;
	after 5 wrong attempts, increment retry bar time by (n-5) * 5 min, where n is number of attempts.
	max out at 24 hours. reset on success.

Assets Table strategy:
	- Identity table: Guid, ModifiedUtc, CreatedUtc, FileSize, SHA256, Blake3, Crumbs
	- LocationForwardTable: Guid -> [paths],
	- LocationReverseTable: path -> Guid,
	- MetadataTable: Guid, Extension, MimeType, ExtraData-JsonObject,
	- VideoTable: Guid, PrimaryVCodec, FrameCount, FrameHeight, FrameWidth, PlaybackInfo,
	- AudioTable: Guid, Codec, Bitrate, Sampling, PlaybackInfo,
	- ImageTable: Guid, Height, Width, PlaybackInfo,
	- DescriptionTable: Guid, Title, Description, Tags, Lazy-Rating, etc.

	Note: For videos,
	Audio goes in audio table,
	but playback-info will be used from video table.
